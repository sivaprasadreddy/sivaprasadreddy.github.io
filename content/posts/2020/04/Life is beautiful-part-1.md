4 months ago...it's 3:00PM and I just came out of this monthly TownHall meeting...I am tired of this pretentious BS..I can't stand it anymore..I need to take a break...that's it.
I am going to take a couple of weeks off and I am going to tell my manager right now.

Me: Rakesh, I need to talk to you.
Rakesh: Sure, tell me.
Me: I want couple of weeks leave.
Rakesh: 2 weeks...no way..we are about to start a new project and I want you to be there. Now is not the right time for a vacation.
Me: Rakesh, it's important for me. I need this time for myself. I am so stressed out right now and I can't take it anymore.
I am going to take 2 weeks leave starting from tomorrow. If you want to fire me for this please go ahead.

I reached home around 6.30PM and lay down on bed thinking what is going on in my life!!!
Too many thoughts are going on in my mind and I am exhausted..I need to take a break..I want to be alone for some time.
I told my wife that I want to go to some place and be alone for few days. Because my wife understands me very well and she knows that lately I have been going through some tough time, she agreed and even booked flight tickets for me to Ooty. Next day I left to Ooty in the morning 7:00AM flight.

I reached the hotel in Ooty around 11:00AM, took shower, had lunch and went to bed. I woke up at around 5.45PM and I just wanted to roam around. Ooty is a very wonderful to see with full of greenery and hills.

After 15 minutes I noticed some another person sitting near by place enjoying the sunset. We both looked at each other and exchanged a wishing smile and I left the place and walked to my hotel room.

---------------

Next day I woke up at 5:00AM and went for walk. It was cold, foggy and looks fantastic. 

<p align="center">
<img src="/images/walking-in-fog-2.jpg"  alt="How could you do that??!!!" />

I keep walking on the road and I saw the same person I saw yesterday. Looks like he is also going for a walk. He smiled at me and I said hello and we started walking together. I introduced myself (I am Sudheer, working as a software engineer, working in Hyderabad blah blah blah) and he introduced himself. His name is Manohar and he is also a Software Engineer working in Bangalore.

Me: You came here alone or with somebody else?
Manohar: I came here alone and I come here once in every 6 or 9 months. Just to relax for few days.
Me: Oh nice.
Manohar: What about you? You came with someone?
Me: No, I also came here alone for few days just to relax.
We keep walking...

Me: Actually I came here to leave the troubles behind and to forget about the shitty workplace at least for few days.
Manohar: hmmmm...that's common for some people working in IT companies.
Me: Is it so common??!!!
Manohar: Not everybody can digest and adjust to the nasty corporate culture. Too much pretentiousness, plastic smiles and loads of lies. It's not easy to adjust to these kind of environments.
Me: Exactly, you just described what I am going through. Actually I took 2 weeks of leave to stay away from the toxic workplace. You also gone through these troubles? 
Manohar: Of course, most of the people go through this. At some point of time I got burnet out and I took 3 months break to sort it out.
Me: So, How did you deal with it?
Manohar: Hmmm..it's a long story...if you don't have any other plans for this evening we can meet and have a drink.
Me: Sure.

---------------
Around 6PM I went to Manohar's hotel room. We sit in balcony and he ordered some drinks and food.
He showed me some of the pics he took while visiting near by places and recommended some must see places.

Manohar: So, tell me your story...why are you so frustrated with the workplace? Is it a hectic job?

Me: No, it's not hectic job. I am not a genius or anything, but I honestly never felt stressed because of the volume of work. I consider myself a decent developer with sufficient skills to get the job done.

Manohar: Then, what is bothering you?

Me: On one hand I feel there are no honest conversations between people in IT companies anymore. When someone is talking nicely, we need to think about what could be their motive. Based on my experience in IT companies I hardly believe anything told by top level management.

On the other hand, there are no normal people anymore. Everyone seems to believe they born to change the world. Thanks to all these Personality Development and Self Help books. People come to the conclusion that "Because they are born to make this world a better place, if they are not spending every minute of free time to sharpen their skills then they are wasting the life".

When everyone around you believes that and try to put every second of our life to a greater purpose then we too start feeling may be we are wasting our time, we should properly manage our time.

But deep down I feel like "Screw all this crap and just simplify live your life".
Does everything we do should turn into a benefit for the job or somehow useful to make money? Shouldn't we do something just for fun? Or can't we just be lazy for a day doing nothing but chilling?

Manohar: I know what you are saying. And, I have gone through all these psychological dilemmas myself. After a lot of thinking and self introspection I kind of manage to reduce these overwhelming thoughts.

I can share with you how I dealt with it, but let me tell you one thing. What worked for me may not work for you.
You need to think what is applicable for you and think for yourself.

Me: Sure.

Manohar:



Me: On one hand, the company's top level management keep telling you lot of things and most of them are lies. They say employee satisfaction is highest priority for them and you clearly know its not at all case looking at the ground reality. They say they are committed to delivering the high quality software but the reality is they don't give a damn about the quality and all they care is to meet deadlines. They say they promote gender equality etc etc and give lot of lectures in the public forums but the reality is it's all part of publicity and marketing stunt. And then there is next level management people who are just "Yes boss" types. Whatever top level management says these "Yes boss" batch applauds and dance to their tunes.

I can understand that while running a big corporate company there are lot of things to do in a diplomatic way and we can't speak honestly all the times. But looking at how easily they lie without any discomfort, you will stop believing anything that comes out of their mouths.
