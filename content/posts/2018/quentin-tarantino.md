I love watching movies, that doesn't mean I watch every film that is released but I watch the films I like again and again. 
I never feel bored of watching my favorite films. Just to give a perspective of what I mean by that, I might have watched 
Pulp Fiction atleast 300 times by now. If you are thinking don't I have anything more important than watching films, I do. 
<!--more-->

I just don't watch films for fun, I study them. I watch them again and again to understand what director might want to tell you, not what is just projected on screen. If you ever try to write a story, give a speech at a conference then you will realize that you will hardly convey 60% of what you imagined in your mind.
Those who knew me personally know that I am a huge Quentin Tarantino fan. Many people even asked me what is so special about him. But it is very hard to convey few things. I will try my level best to explain why I like him and his films so much in this article.

Those who don't know who Quentin Tarantino is, he is a Hollywood film director, actor and more importantly great writer. If you read about him on Wikipedia you will get an impression of him like "He is a very talented director, his films are so violent, dialogues in his films are amazing, he is a bit conterversial person etc". All of them are true.

Let me tell you how violent his films are. Me and my wife were watching The Hateful Eight movie and after seeing Major Marquis Warren shooting Jody Domergue in head my wife ran to wash room as she almost feel vomitting sensation!!!.

One more thing is the language used in his films. You can easily count the words "Fuck", "Mother Fucker" etc using atleast 250+ times in each movie.

With that being said, if you can go beyond that it is unexplainable joy watching his films. If you are a music lover then you will definitely fall in love with his films. Take Pulp Fiction, if you closely observe there is great background music running through out the movie. No one knows better than Tarantino when to use what kind of music. For example, watch Django killing Brittle brothers and notice the amazing background music.

By now you might have noticed one thing, I am referring the people with their character names in the movie instead of their real names. Yes, after watching Tarantine movie you will only see the characters, you forget their real names.

Okay, putting aside my passion about movies for a minute, let me tell you about myself briefly.
I am a software developer by profession. But I read books a lot, especially regarding life, psychology, philosophy etc. I came from a lower middle class family, I am an introvert. My family and couple of my very close friends..that's all my life till I enter into IT field.

After few of starting my career I joined a company where I first realized how cunning some of the people are. Somebody who acted as if he is my friend dig holes behind me and put me into lot of troubles. I am not born smart and I am not dump either, so I slowly realize what that guy is doing. In the beginning I got angry at him but slowly I kind of surprised looking at his manipulation skills. He is 3 or 4 years younger to me and joined IT after 2 year I joined, yet he is fucking awesome at playing politics, getting his things done. Putting aside whether what he did is right or wrong, I really admire his skills.
I left the company because I didn't know how to deal with those kind people and shitty politics. But since then I developed this habbit of studying people behaviour. It's a never ending process. Every person is a unique person and by the time you feel like "Now I know how to deal with these assholes", you will encounter a new type of personality and new troubles.

So, this is how I became a keen observer of people behaviour, why people do certain things the way they do, what is the motive behind certain actions etc.

I have this habbit of once I watch a good movie I will explore that directors other movies and read about him. 

First I watch Kill Bill Volume 1 in television, it is fantastic and then watched Volume 2 and after watching the Climax scene where Bill talk about Super Heros mythology..that's it. I decided to watch the directors rest of the movies. Before that I read about Quentin Tarantine on Wikipedia and watched some of his interviews on youtube. He is a very very very different person.
