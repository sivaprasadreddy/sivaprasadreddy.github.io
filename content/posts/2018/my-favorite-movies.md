# My Favorite Hollywood Movies

I am a big fan of [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino) and I love all of his movies.
But the following movies are top-notch in my opinion and I never get bored with watching them.

* [Pulp Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulp_Fiction)
* [Django Unchained](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Django_Unchained)
* [Kill Bill: Volume 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_Bill:_Volume_1)
* [Kill Bill: Volume 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_Bill:_Volume_2)
* [Inglourious Basterds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inglourious_Basterds)
* [The Hateful Eight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hateful_Eight)

Next, I am huge fan of [Clint Eastwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_Eastwood) and I love his movies too.
Following are my favorite movies acted in or directed by Clint Eastwood.

* [The Good, the Bad and the Ugly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Good,_the_Bad_and_the_Ugly)
* [Million Dollar Baby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Million_Dollar_Baby)
* [Gran Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Torino)
* [The Bridges of Madison County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bridges_of_Madison_County_(film))
