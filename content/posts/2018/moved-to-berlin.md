After dreaming of going abroad for several years, finally I moved to Berlin this July 10th.
Though I wanted to go abroad, I never really put much effort to get visa and go abroad.
But as usually, once I lost hope of ever going to abroad I got a job in one of good company in Berlin.
After going through all visa processing ceremony, I got the visa and flew to Berlin on July 10th along with my wife.

Like many others, I googled and watched a lot of videos about what to do and what not to do in Germany/Berlin,
what are the good places to visit etc.

I thought of sharing my impression of first few weeks in Berlin.

## Public Transportation is amazing
If someone asks me about what is best part of Germany/Berlin, the first thing that comes to my mind is **Public Transportation**.
The entire Berlin city is well connected and you travel to any place using Bus, Train or Tram without using Taxi which is very expensive.
In addition to that, we can take one day ticket or monthly ticket and you can travel in any of bus, train or trams using same ticket.

![Public Transport Train](/images/berlin-train.jpg)

## Beautiful Places to visit
There are plenty of beautiful places to visit in Berlin.
Last week I went to Alexanderplatz and saw the TV Tower and Berlin Dom.

![Berlin Dom](/images/berlin-dom.jpg)

![Berlin TV Tower](/images/berlin-tvtower.jpg)

## Greenery and graffiti everywhere
One thing I liked very much here is we can see greenery everywhere. Very pleasant to eyes.

![Berlin Greenery](/images/berlin-greenery.jpg)

Also, we can see beautiful graffiti all over the city.

![Berlin Graffiti](/images/berlin-graffiti.jpg)

## Few annoying things

* Too much of smoking in public
* After trying couple of Indian restaurants, I lost hope of finding real Indian food
* Be ready to do paperwork for almost everything

Overall it's a wonderful place and plenty of things to see :-)
